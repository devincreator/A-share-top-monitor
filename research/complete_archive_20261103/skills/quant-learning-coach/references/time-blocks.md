# Time Blocks

Use the user's available time to control scope. Keep tasks small enough to finish in-session or immediately after.

## 15 Minutes

Goal: spark curiosity and build one mental hook.

Structure:
- 1 China-market puzzle.
- 1 prediction question.
- 1 core concept.
- 1 judgment question.

Good tasks:
- Explain why a high-return strategy can still be poor after drawdown.
- Compare simple return and log return with a two-day example.
- Judge whether a signal might contain future information.

## 30 Minutes

Goal: finish one compact concept and one exercise.

Structure:
- Case puzzle.
- Short explanation.
- One small calculation or pseudocode exercise.
- Active recall.

Good tasks:
- Calculate ETF return, volatility, and drawdown.
- Explain why signal execution should be delayed one bar.
- Compare CSI 300 and CSI 500 as benchmarks.

## 45 Minutes

Goal: connect concept, data, and a small implementation step.

Structure:
- Case reasoning.
- 1-3 guided questions.
- Code or spreadsheet-style calculation.
- Check result and summarize.

Good tasks:
- Implement max drawdown from a net value series.
- Build a moving-average signal with next-day execution.
- Estimate turnover for monthly ETF rotation.

## 60 Minutes

Goal: complete a small research loop.

Structure:
- Case puzzle.
- Concept explanation.
- Code experiment or manual research design.
- Result interpretation.
- Review note.

Good tasks:
- Backtest one broad-index ETF timing rule.
- Compare 20/60/120-day momentum windows.
- Add transaction costs and benchmark comparison.

## 90+ Minutes

Goal: complete a mini project.

Structure:
- Research question.
- Hypothesis.
- Data and assumptions.
- Signal and portfolio rule.
- Backtest or detailed implementation plan.
- Risk and failure analysis.
- Written research note.

Good tasks:
- ETF momentum rotation among CSI 300, CSI 500, ChiNext, STAR-related ETFs.
- Dividend/low-vol factor analysis.
- Index timing with moving averages and drawdown controls.
- A-share factor grouping exercise.
